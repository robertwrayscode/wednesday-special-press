About

A small presswith a long view.

Wednesday Special Press is the publishing house of Wednesday Special, LLC, an independent company in Houston, Texas.

What we make

We make art books the slow way: close to the artist, designed and edited in house, printed on heavy paper and bound to last. Our first title, Groundswell, is a monograph on the sculptor Patrick Renner.

We also publish lighter projects. Fun Books is our series of illustrated humor books in the style of 1950s how-to manuals.

Who we are

Wednesday Special was founded by Lauri Wray and Robert Wray. Lauri is the publisher and leads every project; Robert handles design, layout and photography.
